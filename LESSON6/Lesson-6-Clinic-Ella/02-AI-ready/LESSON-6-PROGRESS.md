2. **Invest in customer loyalty** - Low marketing spend + high reviews = opportunity for referral formalization

4. **Consider revenue diversification** - Product sales (skincare line) could add 10-15% to margins
5. **Target 2026 projection** - ₪400K+ annual revenue achievable with modest marketing and loyalty push
