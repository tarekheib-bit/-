# Business Asset Taxonomy
## Complete guide to classifying business documents

---

## 1. הנהלת-חשבונות (Accounting & Financials)
**What it includes:**
- Monthly/yearly accounting ledgers and statements
- Income/expense reports (דוח הוצאות, דוח הכנסה)
- General ledger exports
- Trial balances and balance sheets
- Reconciliation files
- Tax reports and declarations
- Cost analysis and budgeting

**File formats:**
- Excel: `.xlsx`, `.xls` (most common - accounting software exports)
- PDF: statements, reports
- CSV: tabular data exports
- Text: journal entries, memo records

**Software that produces these:**
- Zahav, Tallam, Neto (Israeli accounting software)
- QuickBooks, Xero
- Bank export functions (CSV, OFX)

**Tell-tale signs:**
- Column headers like: תאריך, חשבון, סה"כ, ח"ן (debit), ז"ן (credit)
- File names: "חשבונות 2024", "הנהלה", "דוח_הוצאות"

---

## 2. בנקאות-ואשראי (Banking & Credit)
**What it includes:**
- Bank statements (דפי בנק / תנועות בנק)
- Credit card statements (אשראי)
- Loan documents
- Credit lines and limits
- Bank transfers and wire records
- Reconciliation between books and bank

**File formats:**
- PDF: bank statements, credit statements
- CSV/Excel: bank exports, transaction lists
- OFX, QBO: bank format files

**Software:**
- Bank export functions (Bank Hapoalim, Leumi, Discount, Israel Post Bank)
- Accounting software imports

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "דף בנק", "אשראי", "משיכה", "הפקדה"
- Date ranges, account numbers, balance figures
- Fields: סכום, תאריך, תיאור, יתרה

---

## 3. שכר-ומשאבי-אנוש (Payroll & HR)
**What it includes:**
- Payroll records (שכר עובדים)
- Employee contracts (חוזי עבודה)
- Attendance records
- Leave requests
- Benefits documentation
- Employee agreements and policies
- Training records

**File formats:**
- Excel: payroll sheets, HR databases
- PDF: contracts, policies, certificates
- Word: agreements, handbooks

**Software:**
- HRM systems (e.g., Paycom, Workdays)
- Manual Excel tracking

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "שכר", "צוות", "עובדים", "חוזה"
- Column headers: שם עובד, תעודת זהות, משכורת, מס"ה, ביטוח לאומי

---

## 4. מכירות-ולקוחות-CRM (Sales & Customer Relationship Management)
**What it includes:**
- Customer database (לקוחות)
- Supplier contact lists
- Deals/opportunities pipeline
- Customer communications
- Service delivery records
- Customer feedback and complaints
- Pricing packages and bundles

**File formats:**
- Excel: customer lists, deal tracking
- CSV: contact exports from CRM systems
- Word/PDF: customer agreements
- Emails, chat logs

**Software:**
- CRM: Monday.com, Pipedrive, HubSpot (exports to CSV/Excel)
- Zoho CRM, Salesforce
- Custom Excel databases

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "לקוחות", "חבילות", "כרטסת", "ספקים", "הצעה"
- Column headers: שם לקוח, טלפון, דוא"ל, סכום עסקה, סטטוס

---

## 5. תפעול-ERP (Operations & Enterprise Resource Planning)
**What it includes:**
- Inventory management (מלאי)
- Stock tracking (ספירת מלאי)
- Supplier orders
- Production schedules
- Facility operations
- Maintenance logs
- Operational procedures and workflows

**File formats:**
- Excel: inventory sheets, operation logs
- PDF: procedures, manuals
- Word: process descriptions

**Software:**
- ERP systems: SAP, NetSuite, Odoo
- Inventory management: Cin7, TraceLink
- Custom Excel systems

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "מלאי", "ספירה", "הזמנות", "תהליך", "נוהל"
- Column headers: קוד מוצר, כמות, מחסן, יחידה

---

## 6. משפטי-וחוזים (Legal & Contracts)
**What it includes:**
- Employment contracts
- Customer agreements (Terms & Conditions)
- Supplier contracts
- Service agreements
- Compliance documents
- Privacy policies
- Legal correspondence
- Insurance policies and claims

**File formats:**
- Word: `.docx`, `.doc` (primary format)
- PDF: signed documents, official records

**Software:**
- Word (most common)
- Legal templates, document management systems
- Compliance tracking tools

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "חוזה", "הסכם", "תנאים", "מדיניות", "הסכמה"
- Contains: חוקי, אחריות, תוקף, חתימה

---

## 7. שיווק-ומותג (Marketing & Branding)
**What it includes:**
- Brand guidelines (מיתוג - צבעים, פונטים)
- Logo files (לוגו)
- Marketing materials (חוברות, עלונים)
- Presentations (מצגות)
- Advertising copy
- Social media content
- Campaigns
- Photos/visuals of services/products
- Promotional videos

**File formats:**
- Image: `.png`, `.jpg`, `.gif` (logos, photos)
- PDF: brand guidelines, flyers
- PowerPoint: presentations
- Video: `.mp4`, `.mov`
- Word: marketing copy

**Software:**
- Canva, Adobe (Photoshop, Illustrator)
- Design tools
- Figma

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "לוגו", "מיתוג", "צבע", "פונט", "פלייר", "קמפיין", "תמונה"
- Folders: לוגואים/, ויזואלי/, תמונות/

---

## 8. דיגיטל-ולינקים (Digital Assets & Links)
**What it includes:**
- Domain registrations and management
- Website hosting information
- Social media accounts (logins, access info)
- Email account information
- Cloud storage links (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive)
- API keys and integrations
- Web analytics (Google Analytics setup)
- Digital tools and SaaS subscriptions

**File formats:**
- Text: `.txt` (lists, credentials - SECURE CAREFULLY)
- Excel: account inventories
- Word: documentation

**Storage:**
- Usually a separate inventory document or password manager

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "קישור", "אתר", "דומיין", "אימייל", "חשבון"
- Content: URLs, usernames, domain registrars, hosting providers

---

## 9. תקשורת-וביקורות (Communications & Reviews)
**What it includes:**
- Customer testimonials (המלצות)
- Online reviews (ביקורות)
- Review platform exports (Google, Facebook, Trustpilot)
- Email archives
- Marketing communications
- Press releases
- Media coverage

**File formats:**
- CSV/Excel: review exports
- PDF: press releases
- Word: testimonials, case studies
- Screenshots: review screenshots
- Email: correspondence

**Software:**
- Review aggregation platforms (Trustpilot, Google Reviews)
- CRM exports of customer feedback
- Email platforms

**Tell-tale signs:**
- File names: "ביקורות", "המלצות", "דירוגים", "ביקורת"
- Contains: מרצים, דירוגים, משוב לקוח

---

## 10. לא-מסווג (Unclassified)
**What it includes:**
- Files that don't fit other categories
- Ambiguous or mixed-purpose documents
- Old files with unclear purpose
- Duplicates with unclear originals
- Archive files (old/obsolete)

**Action:**
- These are surfaced for manual review
- Review by category specialists to re-classify
- May be deleted if truly obsolete (with approval)
- Or sorted into a "Misc" or "Archive" subfolder

---

## Classification Decision Tree

1. **Is it money-related?**
   - Accounting numbers, ledgers, invoices → **הנהלת-חשבונות**
   - Bank statements, credit cards → **בנקאות-ואשראי**

2. **Is it about people?**
   - Payroll, contracts, HR → **שכר-ומשאבי-אנוש**

3. **Is it about customers or business relationships?**
   - Customer lists, deals → **מכירות-ולקוחות-CRM**
   - Reviews, testimonials → **תקשורת-וביקורות**

4. **Is it about the business itself?**
   - Logo, colors, design → **שיווק-ומותג**
   - Contracts, policies → **משפטי-וחוזים**
   - Inventory, operations → **תפעול-ERP**

5. **Is it about the digital business?**
   - Domains, accounts, links → **דיגיטל-ולינקים**

6. **None of the above?**
   - → **לא-מסווג**

---

## Collection Fallback Chain

When a category is **missing** (e.g., no logo files exist):

1. **Search the archive** - Look through "לא-מסווג" or adjacent categories
2. **Check the source** - Did it exist in the original backup?
3. **Ask the stakeholder** - "We don't have logo files. Where should we collect them from?"
4. **Offer alternatives** - "If logos don't exist as files, can we create a list of where they live?" (e.g., designer's Figma, brand guidelines PDF)
5. **Document the gap** - Record in the catalog what's missing and why

---

## Best Practices

- **Trust the content, not the filename.** A file named "סופי סופי 2.xlsx" in the מלאי folder is inventory, even if the name is opaque.
- **One file, one category.** If a file is truly multi-category (e.g., a contract about payroll), put it where its primary purpose is.
- **Preserve originals.** 00-מקור-גולמי/ is never touched; only copies are organized.
- **The catalog is ground truth.** If the catalog says it's in מיתוג, that's the decision.
